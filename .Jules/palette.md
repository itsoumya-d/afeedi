## 2024-05-18 - [Add Tooltips to IconButtons]
**Learning:** In Flutter apps, tooltips on IconButtons serve a dual purpose: they provide visual hints for sighted users on hover or long-press, while also acting as crucial semantic labels (similar to ARIA labels in HTML) for screen readers, enhancing both usability and accessibility simultaneously without cluttering the UI.
**Action:** When creating or reviewing UI code, especially for icon-only buttons like back buttons or visibility toggles, always ensure a `tooltip` property is defined to maintain accessibility standards.
