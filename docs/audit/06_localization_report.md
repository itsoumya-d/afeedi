## Localization Readiness Score: 8.5 / 10

**Strengths:** 100% key coverage across all 12 languages; proper ARB infrastructure; RTL marker; runtime locale switching with persistence.

**Gaps:** Individual screens still use some hardcoded English strings not yet wrapped in `AppLocalizations.of(context)?.key`; no automated locale-switching widget tests; date/number formatting not yet locale-aware (uses `intl: ^0.20.2` but `DateFormat`/`NumberFormat` not applied to displayed dates in feed cards).

---

## Recommended Next Steps

1. Add widget tests for Arabic (RTL) and at least one LTR non-English locale
2. Wrap remaining hardcoded strings in screens that predated the localization work
3. Apply `DateFormat.yMMMd(locale)` to published-at dates in feed cards and history tiles
4. Apply `NumberFormat.compact(locale)` to view/like counts (e.g., "1.2K" vs "1,2K" in German)
