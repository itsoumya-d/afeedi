## 2024-05-31 - [Adding tooltips to icon-only buttons]
**Learning:** In Flutter, icon-only `IconButton` widgets without a `tooltip` lack semantic labels, making them inaccessible to screen readers and difficult to understand without hover text.
**Action:** Always add a descriptive `tooltip` property to `IconButton` widgets that only contain icons, ensuring they are screen-reader friendly and provide clear visual hints.
